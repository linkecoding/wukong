- [Shared design system](../../design.md) and [portable color/type tokens](../../tokens.css).

Concept A, Cobalt, is the selected identity. The mark combines twin blue headband arcs with a geometric monkey mask on navy. Blue expresses the app’s active controls. The eyes remain legible at small sizes. Keep the mark upright and preserve space of at least one eye-width around it. Do not use the full-color launcher icon as a monochrome Android notification glyph.

## Introduction copy

### English — default

**Name:** Wukong

**Tagline:** Your words. Your phone in action.

**Short description:** An Android AI agent with built-in activation, on-device OCR, virtual-display previews, and manual takeover.

Wukong turns a natural-language goal into actions across your Android phone. Connect your own model, watch the agent work across apps, and take over whenever you need to. Wireless ADB pairing and the shell execution engine are built in—no root, enabled AccessibilityService, or companion app is required. This early preview includes on-device OCR, virtual-display controls, local run logs, and confirmation for actions identified as sensitive. Model requests go to your configured provider. A Codex skill / MCP bridge is planned.

### 简体中文

**名称：** 悟空 Wukong

**标语：** 一句话，让手机行动。

**简短介绍：** Android AI 操作助手，内置激活、本地 OCR、虚拟屏预览，随时接管。

悟空把自然语言目标变成 Android 手机上的实际操作。接入自己的模型，看它跨应用执行任务，也可以随时接管。无线 ADB 配对和 shell 执行引擎全部内置，无需 root、启用无障碍服务或安装外部工具。当前预览版支持本地 OCR、虚拟屏控制、运行日志，并对识别出的敏感操作请求确认。模型请求会发送到用户配置的服务；Codex skill / MCP 接入仍在规划中。

## Claims to preserve

Use “early preview” until a release is validated. Do not describe the agent as fully offline or universally compatible. Do not claim the planned skill bridge is implemented. The source license remains to be selected; avoid “open-source” until it is. Third-party notices continue to apply.
